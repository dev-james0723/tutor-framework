# Public occupation packs

Occupation packs are declarative capability and teaching boundaries. They do not
grant tool access, professional authority, consent, or permission to perform
external writes. A pack should describe drafts, learning exercises, evidence
requirements, supported locales, and the connectors it may request.

Start from [`template/manifest.template.json`](template/manifest.template.json).
Family manifests map the 27 occupation groups in the accompanying guide to 12
low-level extension families. Common packs are reusable workflows that can be
attached to more than one family.
